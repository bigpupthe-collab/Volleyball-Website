Official Rules and Scoring System

Understanding the official rules of volleyball ensures fair play and keeps matches fast, safe, and competitive. All professional and international matches adhere to the standard regulations set by the FIVB.

The Rally Point Scoring System

Modern volleyball utilizes the Rally Point System. This means a point is awarded on every single rally, regardless of which team served the ball. Official matches are typically played as a best-of-five sets:

• Sets 1 to 4 are played to 25 points, requiring a minimum two-point margin to win.

• The deciding fifth set is played to 15 points.

Core Gameplay Rules & Violations

1. Maximum Three Touches: Each team is allowed a maximum of three hits (typically a bump, set, and spike) to return the ball over the net. A block touch does not count as one of the three hits.

2. No Consecutive Hits: A single player cannot hit the ball twice in succession (except immediately after blocking an opponent's attack).

3. Clean Contact: The ball must be cleanly hit, bumped, or set. Catching, holding, lifting, or throwing the ball is classified as a fault.

4. Net & Line Faults: Players are strictly prohibited from touching the top tape of the net during active play. Servers must also remain behind the baseline until striking the ball.
