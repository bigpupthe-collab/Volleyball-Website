Key Player Positions on the Court

A standard indoor volleyball team consists of six players on the court at any given time, divided into three front-row players and three back-row players. Each position has a specialized role that contributes to both offense and defense.

Detailed Position Breakdown

Setter: Often considered the playmaker or quarterback of the team. The setter's primary job is to deliver accurate overhead passes to the attackers so they can spike effectively.

Outside Hitter: The primary offensive attacker who hits balls set to the left antenna and acts as a key blocker.

Middle Blocker: Positioned in the center of the front row, using height and quick footwork to block opponent spikes and execute fast attacks.

Libero: A dedicated back-row defensive specialist who wears a contrasting jersey color and excels at passing and digging.
