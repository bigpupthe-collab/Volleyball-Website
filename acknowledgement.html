Brief History of Volleyball

Volleyball was invented on February 9, 1895, by William G. Morgan at the YMCA in Holyoke, Massachusetts, USA. Originally named "Mintonette", it was designed as a less intense indoor sport before expanding globally and debuting at the 1964 Tokyo Olympics.
